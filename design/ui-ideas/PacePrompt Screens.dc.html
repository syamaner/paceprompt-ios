2e Architecture note
If this is ever built
•A future Watch companion may own the primary HKWorkoutSession and provide heart rate and active energy.
•The iPhone remains the treadmill orchestration authority.
•The Watch must not infer command success or treadmill state.
•Watch availability is never required for physical safety.
•The iPhone and Watch must not create duplicate HealthKit workouts.
•Loss of Watch or phone connectivity must not trigger automatic treadmill commands, reconnection or workout resumption.

Fourteen screens, one grammar

Plans in, validated locally, nothing written to the machine. Every status below is drawn from the same five-tone vocabulary, and unknown never borrows the look of confirmed. Reference screens by id in chat — 1c, 1m, and so on.
