## Exact native sample eligibility

The proposed sample remains precisely the existing accepted aggregate and unchanged assembly interval. A positive-duration overlap between that interval and any native paused period makes it ineligible, including an open final pause whose start precedes the rounded sample end by a fraction of a millisecond. Use full native Date precision; no epsilon, time rounding or tolerance can hide this case. A pause beginning exactly at sample end contributes no overlap.

Map genuine pause/resume transitions; require monotonic coherent events and a known initial running state. Unknown pause-affecting events, malformed/unbalanced sequences, missing boundary evidence or inability to prove collection-bound validity are unavailable rather than assumed safe. Marker/lap/segment events do not become pauses. Motion pause/resume and request events require an explicit SDK-supported mapping, otherwise fail closed. Final open pause extends at least through the proposed end. Both ordinary mid-workout and final sub-ms pauses must be covered by pure and real-native tests. One narrow native-proven exception permits a final genuine point pause after collectionEnd when the exact existing WatchWire canonical-millisecond projection of its full-precision date equals collectionEnd. It must be the final event, prior trace must be coherent and proposed sample bounds strictly valid with zero pause overlap. No other out-of-bounds event is accepted. This preserves the observed native final pause occurring just after its rounded collection end without shifting dates or an epsilon. A pause before sample end remains overlap and is suppressed.

HealthKit27 SDK HKWorkoutBuilder.addSamples documentation says sample start must be later than receiver start. Require finite dates, proposed start strictly greater than exact builder.startDate, proposed end at or before known builder.endDate, and start strictly before end. Missing or invalid bounds suppress with uncertainTemporalCoverage. Never shift a timestamp. Map pause/resume as coherent ordered transitions with known initial running state; open final pause extends through the proposed end. Lap, marker and segment do not pause; motionPaused, motionResumed, pauseOrResumeRequest and unknown types fail closed as uncertainTemporalCoverage. Preserve existing !hasDistance && sourceExcludesDistance assembly preconditions: unexpected native distance or absent exclusion still fails before finish, without a new suppression reason or relaxed source assurance. Accepted aggregate survives authorised suppression, not an invalid assembly.

## User-visible limitation

When a new v3 workout cannot safely represent its accepted aggregate as one native duration-spanning sample, PacePrompt omits that native sample. Apple Health and Fitness may therefore show no native distance for that workout. WeeklyHealthReport schema7 preserves the separate accepted treadmill total and the explicit writer decision; this does not claim that Apple Health or Fitness displays the accepted total. Historical Health1/2 sample-writing semantics stay unchanged. Legacy recovery reads the original associated sample and never rewrites the workout or its native statistics.
